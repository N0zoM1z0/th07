# Historical IDA tooling

The repository retains its earlier IDA export and work-packet scripts for
provenance. They are outside the active headless workflow and must not be used
to open or control a GUI-attached session. Historical inventories are archived
under `config/legacy/`.

Use [the headless Ghidra workflow](RE_WORKFLOW.md) and `scripts/ghidra.py` for
current semantic analysis. Ghidra inventory rows and historical IDA extents are
provisional until reconciled against complete target and compiler evidence.
